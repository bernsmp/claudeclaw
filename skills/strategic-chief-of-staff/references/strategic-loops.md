# Strategic Loops

Use the smallest loop that fits the moment.

## 1. Relationship Intelligence

Use when:

- a client or prospect is warming
- a relationship is drifting
- a follow-up window opened
- a do-not-touch condition matters

Output:

- relationship state
- best next move
- timing reason
- caution if needed

## 2. Opportunity Radar

Use when:

- repeated pain suggests productization
- a call creates upsell surface area
- a transcript contains reusable content
- multiple weak signals converge into one strong angle

Output:

- opportunity
- likely lane: revenue, product, content, outbound, leverage
- recommended action

## 3. Attention Defense

Use when:

- Max is reacting instead of steering
- founder time is being spent below its value
- similar low-leverage work is repeating

Output:

- what pattern is happening
- what to stop
- what to do instead

## 4. Weekly Strategic Steering

Use when writing a weekly memo.

Answer:

- where attention leaked
- what created leverage
- what to stop
- what hidden bet is being made
- top 3 moves for the next 7 days

## 5. Pre-Decision Brief

Use before a call, project move, or tradeoff.

Answer:

- what changed
- what matters
- biggest risk
- best move
- second-order effect
